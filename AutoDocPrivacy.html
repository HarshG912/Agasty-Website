1. Introduction & Overview

AutoDoc Studio ("we," "our," or "the Application") is an Android application designed for document automation, interactive form generation, and instant high-fidelity PDF rendering.

We prioritize user privacy and transparency. This Privacy Policy details the types of information collected, processed, and shared when using AutoDoc Studio, and outlines our adherence to the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement and User Data policies.

Effective Date: November 26, 2026 | Last Updated: November 26, 2026

2. Local Document Processing (Zero Data Transmission)

Core Privacy Guarantee: All document form inputs, calculations, personal data, and generated PDF files are rendered locally on your Android device. We do NOT upload, collect, or store your private document contents on external servers.

When generating documents (such as invoices, bills of lading, legal affidavits, certificates, or agreements):

  • Form responses and document contents remain strictly inside your device's sandbox.
  • PDF creation is handled on-device via Android's native print and rendering pipelines.
  • Generated files are saved in your app-specific private directory and are only shared externally when you explicitly initiate an action (e.g., WhatsApp, Gmail, Telegram, or Print via Android Sharesheet).

3. Information We Collect

AutoDoc Studio only collects data necessary to provide optional cloud sync, user authentication, and community publishing features:

A. Optional Account & Profile Data

  • Email Address: Collected if you choose to sign up or sign in using email OTP (One-Time Password) or password credentials.
  • Unique Username: A public display username (e.g., @username) chosen by you to identify your authored suites on the Community Hub.
  • Authentication Tokens: Secure session tokens provided by Supabase to keep you authenticated.

B. Community Hub & Public Contributions

  • Public Document Templates: If you intentionally publish an automation suite to the Community Hub, the title, description, category, tags, form schema, and HTML/CSS template become public for other community members to view, fork, and rate.
  • Ratings & Stars: Community stars and 1-to-5 star ratings you assign to public suites are stored with your unique user ID to compute aggregated community scores.

C. Automatically Collected Technical & Diagnostic Data

Like most Android applications, third-party libraries integrated into the app may automatically collect certain diagnostic information:

  • Device model, manufacturer, and screen resolution.
  • Operating system version and language settings.
  • IP address and coarse network status (Wi-Fi/Cellular).
  • Google Advertising ID (GAID) for advertising attribution.

5. Android Device Permissions

AutoDoc Studio requests only the minimal permissions required for its feature set:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Enables syncing community automation templates, authenticating your account, and fetching advertisements from Google AdMob.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Detects internet availability to gracefully toggle offline and online sync states.
  • FileProvider (Temporary URI Sharing): Enables sending generated PDFs to selected third-party apps (WhatsApp, Gmail, Telegram) without granting broad access to your entire storage drive.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy

In compliance with Google Play's User Data policy on account deletion:

  • Local Data: You can wipe all local document automations, saved drafts, and generated PDFs at any time by clearing the application data in your Android device settings.
  • Cloud Account Deletion: You have the right to request full deletion of your user account, email address, profile, and community contributions.

To request permanent deletion of your account and associated data:

Submit an email request with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - AutoDoc Studio" to:
support@agasty.cloud (or your registered developer contact email).
Include your registered email address and username. Account deletion is executed within 30 days of verification.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

AutoDoc Studio is a document production and workflow tool designed for professional and general productivity use. The Application is not directed toward children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.

8. Data Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security safeguards to protect your information:

  • All cloud API communications are encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Database tables employ Row-Level Security (RLS) policies to prevent unauthorized data tampering.
  • Local files are confined to the application's internal secure storage container.
