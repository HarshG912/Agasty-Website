1. Overview & Commitment

OHE Records ("we", "our", or "the App") is an engineering and field utility application designed for railway traction power engineers, supervisors, and technicians. The App provides tools for measuring Overhead Equipment (OHE) mast deflection using device sensors and camera-based photogrammetry, calculating Traction Sub-Station (TSS) fault tripping distances, storing field records, and managing emergency quota requisitions and automations.

We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, handles, and protects your information when you use the OHE Records mobile application.

2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

A. Account and Authentication Information

When you register or sign in to OHE Records to synchronize your records across devices or access organization features, we collect:

  • Email Address: Used strictly to authenticate your account, secure your profile, and deliver OTP / password recovery verifications.
  • Organization Association: If you join or manage an organization (such as a railway division or board), your membership and delegated role (e.g., Admin, Member) are associated with your account.

B. Mast Deflection & Sensor Data

The App utilizes internal device sensors (Gyroscope, Accelerometer, and Gravity sensors) to determine real-time mast deflection angles:

  • Sensor Readings: Calculated locally in-memory on your device in real-time. Raw sensor streams are never uploaded, logged, or transmitted to any external server.
  • Deflection Records: When you choose to save a measurement (mast location, chainage, track name, measured angle, and calculated deflection in mm), the record is saved locally on your device and, if signed in, synchronized to your organization's secure cloud database.

C. Camera & Mast Photos

The App includes a photo-based deflection measurement tool allowing users to mark reference points on an OHE mast photo:

  • Camera Usage: The camera is used solely when you explicitly tap the "Take Photo" button within the app.
  • Photo Storage: Photos captured or selected from your gallery are processed entirely on your device. Annotated photos are saved to your device's photo gallery only when you explicitly tap "Save Photo". We do not upload or store your photos on our servers.

D. TSS Fault Distance & Tripping Data

Fault distance calculations (relay types, impedance, resistance, fault current, and tripping time) are saved in the cloud only for authenticated team members authorized by their organization administrator. Power delegation controls ensure only authorized personnel can view or record tripping logs.

E. Automations & Document Presets

When utilizing the Emergency Quota (EQ) requisition feature:

  • Form details (train number, journey date, passenger details) are submitted directly to the designated requisition backend without intermediary storage.
  • Letterhead and footer preset images are saved locally in the app's secure private sandbox directory (/data/user/0/com.harsh.deflection/files/automation_presets/) and encoded securely for universal synchronization within authorized organizations.

3. Device Permissions Explained

The App requests only permissions strictly necessary for its technical features:

Permission Purpose
CAMERA Required to take clear photos of OHE masts for photogrammetric deflection measurement.
VIBRATE Provides tactile haptic feedback when a sensor reading is registered via the volume key.
INTERNET Required for cloud synchronization of deflection & tripping records, authentication, and loading banner ads.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Used in Android 13+ to deliver operational notifications, tripping alerts, and workflow updates.
AD_ID Standard Google Play Services Advertising ID permission used by Google AdMob to deliver banner ads.

4. Third-Party Services & Libraries

We partner with trusted third-party providers who may collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic and advertising data in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (Mobile Ads SDK): Displays banner advertisements within the app. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), IP address, and interaction data to serve ads. Learn more: Google Advertising Privacy Policy.
  • Google Firebase: Used for Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver notifications. Learn more: Firebase Privacy and Security.
  • Supabase: Provides enterprise-grade PostgreSQL database infrastructure and JWT-based authentication for team syncing. Communication is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. Learn more: Supabase Privacy Policy.

5. Data Storage, Retention & Security

We implement industry-standard technical measures to safeguard your data:

  • Encrypted Transmission: All data in transit between the App and backend servers is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2/1.3).
  • Sandbox Storage: Photos, presets, and cached files are stored in the App's private internal sandbox, inaccessible to other third-party applications.
  • Access Controls: Organization data is restricted by role-based power delegation set by organizational administrators.
  • Data Retention: We retain your cloud account and field records only for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the App's services.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion
