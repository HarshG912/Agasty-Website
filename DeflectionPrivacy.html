1 Overview & Engineering Scope

OHE Records / Deflection (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) is an engineering utility designed for railway traction power engineers, supervisors, and technicians. The application provides precision tools for measuring Overhead Equipment (OHE) mast deflection using internal smartphone sensors and camera-based photogrammetry, calculating Traction Sub-Station (TSS) fault tripping distances, storing field records, and managing emergency quota requisitions.

2 Information We Collect and How We Use It

A. Mast Deflection & Sensor Processing

The App utilizes internal device sensors (Gyroscope, Accelerometer, and Gravity sensors) to determine real-time mast deflection angles:

  • Sensor Readings: Calculated locally in-memory on your device in real time. Raw high-frequency sensor streams are never uploaded, logged, or transmitted externally.
  • Deflection Records: When you choose to save a measurement (mast location, chainage, track name, measured angle, and calculated deflection in mm), the record is saved locally on your device and, if signed in, synchronized to your organization’s secure cloud database.

B. Camera & Photogrammetry Photos

The App includes a photo-based deflection measurement tool allowing users to mark reference points on an OHE mast photo:

  • The camera is accessed solely when you explicitly tap “Take Photo” inside the application.
  • Photos captured or selected from your gallery are processed entirely on your device. Annotated photos are saved to your device gallery only upon user action. We do not store or transmit your photos to third parties.

C. TSS Fault Distance & Tripping Data

Fault distance calculations (relay types, impedance, resistance, fault current, and tripping time) are saved in the cloud only for authenticated team members authorized by their organization administrator. Power delegation controls ensure only authorized personnel can view or record tripping logs.
