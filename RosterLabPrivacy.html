Privacy Policy for RosterLab

Effective Date: July 26, 2026 • Agasty Enterprises

RosterLab Mobile App (iOS / Android)

RosterLab (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the RosterLab mobile application (the “App”).

1 Information We Collect

A. Personal Account Data

  • Name: To identify you within your organization’s shift roster.
  • Email Address: For authentication, account management, and important roster alerts.
  • Job Role & Organization: To properly assign you to shifts and group communication channels.

B. User-Generated Content

  • Chat Messages: Stored within the team chat feature to facilitate shift handovers and organization communications.
  • Roster & Leave Requests: Any shift preferences, swap bids, and leave submissions.

C. Automatically Collected Device Telemetry

Device type, OS version, and diagnostic usage logs required to maintain application stability.

2 Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

Supabase Backend

Used for encrypted database hosting, JWT authentication, and real-time chat sync.

Google AdMob

May serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements according to your device settings.

3 Data Retention & User Controls

  • Message Deletion: You can delete your sent chat messages within a 5-minute window directly in the interface.
  • Account Deletion: You may request complete account and data removal by contacting your organization admin or reaching out to support@agasty.cloud.
