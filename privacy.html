Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 28, 2026 • Agasty Enterprises

Agasty: N8N Wrapper RailBlock Scan the Table Agasty Cloud Services

Agasty Enterprises (“we,” “our,” or “us”) operates the website agasty.cloud and our suite of enterprise, developer, and mobile applications including RailBlock, Scan the Table, and Agasty: N8N Wrapper (collectively referred to as the “Services”).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and safeguard your personal information when you access, download, or use our applications and cloud endpoints.

1 Information We Collect

A. App-Specific Operational Information

  • Agasty (N8N Mobile Wrapper): Operates as a sandboxed on-device execution environment. Workflows, environment variables, credentials, and automation configurations are processed and stored locally on your device storage. We do not inspect, log, or sell your workflow payloads or custom node data.
  • RailBlock: For administrative, audit, and operational safety purposes, the app collects user-provided operational credentials, such as Employee ID, designation, railway station/section, and digital log entries associated with block and permit operations.
  • Scan the Table: We collect venue business details (name, catalog, table identifiers) and customer order transactions required to generate QR digital menus and process live orders.

B. Automatically Collected Diagnostic & Telemetry Data

When interacting with our services, we may collect minimal technical diagnostics to maintain service reliability:

  • Device hardware model, OS build version, and screen metrics.
  • Network telemetry (anonymized IP routing, crash stack traces, API response latencies).
  • Performance metrics used to optimize node execution and cold-start times.

2 Device Permissions & System Access

  • Foreground Services & Partial Wake Locks: Kept active to maintain local workflow execution, background jobs, and operational notifications while the screen is locked.
  • Camera Permission (Scan the Table): Utilized solely to scan QR codes for table identification. Camera feeds and photos are never uploaded or recorded.
  • Local Storage Access: Required to export workflows, generate backup zip files, and manage runtime logs.
  • Network & Internet Access: Required for tunnel connectivity (FRP/Ngrok), menu synchronization, and entitlement validation.

3 Subscriptions, Payments & Security

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing and RevenueCat SDK. We do not store or process your credit card numbers or banking credentials on our infrastructure.

Zero-Knowledge Secrets: API tokens, database passwords, and webhook secrets configured inside your local instances remain solely under your control on your device.

4 Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

We retain operational records only as long as necessary to fulfill services or statutory railway compliance audits. You have the full right to request total deletion of your personal account data at any time by contacting our data protection officer at support@agasty.cloud.
