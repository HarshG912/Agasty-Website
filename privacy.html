Agasty: N8N Wrapper RailBlock Scan the Table Agasty Cloud Services

Agasty Enterprises ("we," "our," or "us") operates the website agasty.cloud and our mobile and web applications, including RailBlock, Scan the Table, and Agasty: N8N Wrapper (collectively referred to as the "Services").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you install and use our applications and services.

1. Information We Collect

A. App-Specific Information

  • Agasty (N8N Mobile Wrapper): This application operates as an on-device execution environment. Workflows, environment variables, credentials, and automation configurations are processed and stored locally on your device storage. We do not inspect, log, or sell your workflow payloads or custom node data.
  • RailBlock: For administrative, audit, and operational safety purposes, the app may collect user-provided credentials, such as Employee ID, designation, railway station/section, and digital log entries associated with block and permit operations.
  • Scan the Table: We collect restaurant business details (e.g., name, menu catalog, table identifiers) and customer order transactions required to generate QR digital menus and process live orders.

B. Automatically Collected Diagnostic & Technical Data

When you interact with our applications, we may automatically collect diagnostic data to ensure stability, including:

  • Device information (hardware model, operating system version, screen resolution).
  • Network and connection telemetry (IP address, anonymized crash reports, API error logs).
  • Anonymous performance metrics to debug cold-boot sequences and server process execution.

2. Device Permissions & System Access

Depending on the specific application used, we request certain device permissions:

  • Foreground Service & Partial Wake Locks (Agasty / RailBlock): Required to keep local workflow execution engines, background automation jobs, and real-time operational notifications active while the screen is locked.
  • Camera Permission (Scan the Table): Required solely to scan QR codes for table ordering and setup. No camera feeds or images are stored or transmitted.
  • Storage / File Access: Required to export workflows, download digital logs, or manage local runtime directories.
  • Network & Internet Access: Required to establish cloud tunnels (FRP/Ngrok), synchronize menu data, and verify subscription entitlements.

3. Subscriptions, Payments & In-App Purchases

Our applications may offer premium features (e.g., custom domain tunneling, advanced networking configurations, or enterprise features).

  • Google Play Billing & RevenueCat: For in-app purchases processed via the Google Play Store, transactions are secured and managed through Google Play Billing and RevenueCat SDK. We do not store or process your credit card, debit card, or banking details on our servers.
  • External / Direct Payments: If subscriptions are purchased directly through our web portal, payments are securely processed by authorized payment gateways.

Note on Sensitive Workflows: API tokens, database keys, and webhook secrets configured within your local instances remain solely under your control on your device or designated server.

4. Third-Party Services

We may employ trusted third-party service providers to facilitate and monitor our Services:

  • Google Play Services (Core runtime, licensing, and analytics)
  • RevenueCat (In-app purchase verification and receipt validation)
  • Cloudflare & Traefik Infrastructure (Encrypted proxy routing, SSL management, and DDoS protection)

5. Data Security

We utilize industry-standard technical and operational measures, including TLS/HTTPS encryption across all public endpoints, strict privilege isolation, and containerized cloud runtimes to safeguard your data. However, no electronic transmission or local storage system is 100% impenetrable.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain operational data only as long as necessary to fulfill the services requested or comply with regulatory audit requirements.

Your Deletion Rights: You have the right to request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time. To request data deletion, email our data support team at support@agasty.cloud with your registered account identifiers.
